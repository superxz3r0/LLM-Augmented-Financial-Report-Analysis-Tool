Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our lead therapeutic candidates may fail in clinical trials.
Drug development is inherently uncertain. Our two lead candidates are in Phase 2 trials, and adverse safety findings or a failure to demonstrate efficacy would materially harm our prospects, delay or prevent regulatory approval, and could cause significant losses and impairment of our intangible assets.

We depend on a single contract manufacturer for biologics production.
All clinical supply of our biologic candidates is produced by one contract manufacturing organisation. Capacity constraints, quality failures, or contractual disputes could delay our trials and increase costs. Qualifying an alternative manufacturer would take at least eighteen months.

Reimbursement uncertainty could limit the commercial opportunity for approved products.
Even if approved, our products may not achieve adequate pricing or reimbursement from government payers and insurers. Downward pricing pressure across the biotechnology sector creates uncertainty regarding the revenue potential of our pipeline.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Fiscal 2025 was a year of strong scientific progress. We completed enrolment ahead of schedule in both Phase 2 studies and received Fast Track designation for HLX-204 in fibrotic disease, a significant achievement that we believe strengthens our regulatory position. Collaboration revenue increased to $145 million, reflecting a milestone achievement under our partnership with a global pharmaceutical company.

Research and development expenses increased 22 percent to $410 million as trial activity expanded. We expect fiscal 2026 collaboration revenue of $160 million to $190 million, and we anticipate capital expenditures of approximately $55 million, principally for the expansion of our process development laboratories in Dublin.

We ended the year with $1.1 billion in cash, providing runway into fiscal 2028. We remain confident that our differentiated discovery platform represents a substantial opportunity, although success depends on clinical outcomes that remain uncertain.
